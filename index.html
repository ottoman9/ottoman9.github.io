Abaqus/Explicit, scripted DOE in Python

Crash box under oblique impact

Crash boxes are certified head-on, but real crashes arrive at an angle. This study finds the angle where a production-class box stops folding and hinges over (6.25°), traces the cause to the loaded end, and designs a targeted web-and-bead fix, checked by a 25-point designed experiment.
